Empowering Citizens Through Smart Civic Tech

Awam Ki Awaz is an AI-assisted portal designed to bridge the gap between citizens and municipal departments in Karachi. We transform raw voice complaints into structured, department-routed grievances.

Why Awam Ki Awaz? (Key Features)

🎙️

Voice-to-Text Complaint Input

Speak naturally in Urdu or Roman Urdu. Our portal listens, transcribes, and structures your complaint effortlessly.

⚡

Smart AI Auto-Routing

No more guessing department names. The system auto-detects whether your issue belongs to K-Electric, KWSC, SSGC, or KMC.

📝

Formal Summary Generation

Converts everyday conversational voice messages into formal English grievance summaries ready for official submission.

📍

Location Parsing

Intelligently captures area details like block numbers and neighborhood names directly from your voice input.
